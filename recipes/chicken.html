Chicken Quesadilla

Description

Homemade, quick, and easy chicken quesadilla

Ingredients

  • 2 flour tortillas
  • 1 cup cooked shredded chicekn
  • 1 cup shredded cheddar
  • Salsa

Steps

  1. Lay tortilla over skillet
  2. Sprinkle cheese
  3. Add the chicken
  4. Top off with cheese
  5. Place second tortilla on top
  6. Cook for 3 minutes
  7. Flip tortilla
  8. Cook until cheese is melted
