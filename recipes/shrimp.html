Shrimp Alfredo

Description

Quick 30 minute shrimp alfredo recipe

Ingredients

  • 12 oz fettuccine
  • 2 tablespoon olive oil
  • 12 tablespoon salted butter
  • 1 lb jumbo shrimp
  • Kosher salt
  • Black Pepper
  • 1 1/1 cups heavy cream
  • 2 garlic cloves, minced
  • 2 cups grated parmesan
  • 1/2 cup parsley

Steps

  1. Cook pasta according to package
  2. Heat olive oil and butter
  3. Add shrimp
  4. Sprinkle salt and pepper
  5. Cook 2 1/2 minutes while stirring
  6. Remove shrimp
  7. Add heavy cream and remaining butter
  8. Add garlic, shrimp and pasta
  9. Add parmesan and parsley
