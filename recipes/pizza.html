Pizza

Description

Recipe for a homemade pepperoni pizza.

Ingredients

  • Store brought pizza dough
  • 1/2 cup pizza sauce
  • 18 slices of pepperoni
  • 12 ounces of mozzarella cheese
  • 1/2 black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon oregano
  • Flour
  • 1/2 cup tomato sauce
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/2 teaspoon black pepper
  • 1 teaspoon olive oil

Steps

  1. Preheat over to 500 degrees F
  2. Make the sauce
  3. Roll out the dough
  4. Add toppings
  5. Cook pizza
  6. Slice and serve
